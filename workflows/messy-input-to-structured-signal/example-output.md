# Messy Input to Structured Signal

## Objective
Create a reviewable picture of the Site West generator issue and replacement activity without treating informal commentary as established fact.

## Source inventory
- Facilities email: replacement intent and quote status.
- Spreadsheet note: AMBER status, recurring fuel alarm, owner listed as Sam.
- Meeting note: conditional funding approval up to $180k.
- Service ticket INC-7741: four September false alarms and 2 October recalibration.
- Chat message: unverified claim that West is the only affected site.

## Supported facts
- Four false fuel-level alarms were recorded in September for Site West.
- Sensor recalibration was completed on 2 October.
- CFO approved up to $180k subject to final quote.
- Replacement quotes are being sought.

## Candidate structure
| Item | Current signal | Owner | Next evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel sensor issue | Recalibrated after four false alarms | Sam appears in spreadsheet note | Confirm post-recalibration recurrence |
| Generator replacement | Intended before summer; quotes pending | Not established | Final quote and approved delivery date |
| Funding | Up to $180k conditionally approved | CFO approval noted | Final quote / funding confirmation |

## Uncertainty
- It is not established that Site West is the only affected site.
- The spreadsheet's AMBER status criteria are not supplied.
- Sam's exact accountability is not defined.

## Actions and owners
No new actions or owners should be invented. Existing material supports obtaining the final quote and checking post-recalibration alarm history, but ownership/dates require confirmation.

## Review
A human owner should confirm the candidate structure, accountable owner and whether AMBER remains the correct status.
