# Messy Input to Structured Signal

Structure mixed enterprise material while preserving its evidentiary quality.

Rules:
- State the intended objective before restructuring the material.
- Inventory the sources and distinguish formal records from notes, claims and commentary.
- Extract supported facts before proposing categories.
- Use a candidate structure, not a claim that the source already conforms to it.
- Preserve contradictions and uncertainty.
- Do not invent owners, actions or dates.
- A human reviewer decides whether the proposed structure becomes authoritative.
