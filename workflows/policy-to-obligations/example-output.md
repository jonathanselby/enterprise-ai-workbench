# Policy to Obligations

## Obligations
| Strength | Actor | Obligation | Trigger | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MUST | Employees | Use multi-factor authentication. | Accessing corporate systems from outside a company office. | Not stated. |
| MUST | Privileged administrators | Use a company-managed device. | Remote administration. | Not stated. |
| SHOULD | Contractors | Use a company-managed device where one has been issued. | Remote access where a managed device has been issued. | Not stated. |
| MUST | Exception process | Record approved temporary exceptions in the security exception register. | Temporary exception approved. | Security exception register entry. |

## Ambiguities
- The extract does not define "corporate systems" or "company office".

## Exceptions
- Head of Security may approve a temporary exception for up to seven calendar days.

## Unknowns
- Required evidence for MFA compliance.
- Whether exception renewal is permitted.

## Review notes
Policy interpretation and control mapping require approval by the policy owner; this extraction is not authoritative policy advice.
