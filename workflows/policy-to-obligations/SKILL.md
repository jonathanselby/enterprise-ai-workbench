# Policy to Obligations

Extract obligations without rewriting policy intent.

Rules:
- Preserve MUST / SHOULD / MAY strength where stated.
- Do not turn descriptive text into an obligation.
- Record actor/owner only when supported.
- Capture trigger, required evidence and exceptions where stated.
- Quote minimally; reference source wording instead of paraphrasing away ambiguity.
- Flag conflicts and undefined terms.
- A human policy owner remains authoritative for interpretation.
