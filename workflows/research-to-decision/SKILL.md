# Research to Decision

Analyse the supplied material as decision support, not as a narrative summary.

Rules:
- Separate source-supported evidence from inference.
- Identify material contradictions rather than reconciling them silently.
- Say what the evidence does not support.
- Preserve uncertainty.
- Make options genuinely distinct.
- State decision criteria before recommending.
- A recommendation is advisory; the human reviewer remains authoritative.
- Do not invent facts, owners, dates or certainty.

Use the required headings exactly.
