# Question
Should Bafflewick Consolidated replace its ageing warehouse scanners this year or extend them for another 18 months?

# Evidence
- Operations reports 14% of scanners fail at least once per shift.
- Finance has allocated $420,000 for replacement, but the budget expires at financial year end.
- The incumbent vendor says batteries will remain available for three years; no supply commitment was provided.
- A pilot of newer scanners reduced average pick confirmation time from 11 seconds to 7 seconds across 18 operators.
- The pilot ran for two weeks and did not cover the freezer warehouse.
- Cyber has not completed its review of the new Android device management profile.
- Warehouse management software integration worked in the pilot using the existing API.
