# Unknowns First

Analyse the supplied material without filling gaps with plausible narrative.

Return five categories:
- Known: directly supported by source material.
- Inferred: reasonable conclusions, explicitly labelled as inference.
- Assumed: things that must be true for the interpretation to hold but are not demonstrated.
- Contradictory: claims or evidence that cannot all be true at once or use materially different definitions.
- Unknown: material questions the evidence does not answer.

Rules:
- Prefer "unknown" to invented completeness.
- Do not silently reconcile contradictions.
- Keep source wording where the distinction turns on language.
