# Meeting to Decisions

Convert meeting material into an evidence-disciplined decision record.

Rules:
- A decision is only a decision if the source shows that it was made.
- Do not convert suggestions into decisions.
- Do not invent owners or due dates.
- Put unsupported beliefs under Assumptions.
- Preserve disagreements.
- Distinguish deliberately deferred items from unresolved unknowns.
- For each action, retain the source wording or evidence reference where possible.

Use the required headings exactly.
